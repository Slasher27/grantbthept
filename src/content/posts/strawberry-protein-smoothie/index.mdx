## Strawberry-banana protein smoothie

This strawberry-banana protein smoothie with almonds and protein powder is great for a meal replacement or after a workout. For a natural energy drink or a filling breakfast, try whizzing up one of our nutritious smoothies using seasonal fruit and veg.

### Ingredients

- 1 ¼ cups sliced fresh strawberries
- 1 banana
- 10 whole almonds
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1 cup ice cubes
- 3 tablespoons chocolate flavored protein powder

### Directions

1. Place strawberries, banana, almonds, and water into a blender; blend to combine. Add ice cubes and puree until smooth. Add protein powder and mix until evenly incorporated, about 30 seconds.
